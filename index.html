About the Game

A Masterpiece of Storytelling

Early Development Valorant's development started in 2014 under the codename Project A at Riot Games' Los Angeles headquarters. The concept was spearheaded by Joe Ziegler, alongside David Nottingham, Trevor Romleski, Moby Francke, and Salvatore Garozzo, a former professional Counter-Strike: Global Offensive map designer. The team aimed to create a tactical first-person shooter that was accessible to both new players and competitive professionals, focusing on gameplay optimization using Unreal Engine 4 and low hardware requirements to reach a broad audience.

Purpose of creating valorant The purpose of creating Valorant was to make tactical shooters and e-sports more accessible to new players while attracting a large competitive scene. Valorant was developed to prioritize teamwork and tactical cooperation, making it suitable for both casual and competitive players. The game's unique ability system and competitive esports features were designed to cater to different playstyles and preferences, ensuring a diverse and engaging gaming experience. Valorant's development involved extensive market research and feedback, which helped refine the game's core mechanics and features, ultimately shaping its identity as a tactical shooter that combines elements of first-person shooters, tactical strategy, and competitive esports.
